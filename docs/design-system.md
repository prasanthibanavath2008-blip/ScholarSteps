# ScholarSteps Design System

## Concept
"Editorial opportunity discovery meets a calm preparation studio."

## Palette
- **Canvas**: `#F7F8FA` (main), `#FAF9F6` (editorial warm sections)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (cards, panels), `#F0F3F6` (muted grouping)
- **Ink**: `#15263D` (primary text), `#46566B` (secondary text), `#64748B` (metadata)
- **Primary**: `#0B6B63` (teal brand CTA), `#08574F` (hover), `#06463F` (pressed), `#E8F5F1` (tint)
- **Accent**: `#C9F17C` (restrained chartreuse highlight)
- **Border**: `#DFE5EB`, Strong: `#BAC6D2`
- **Semantic**:
  - Success: `#16634F`, Tint: `#EAF7F0`
  - Warning: `#875500`, Tint: `#FFF5DD`
  - Danger: `#B42335`, Tint: `#FFF0F2`
  - Info: `#2457A7`, Tint: `#EFF5FF`

## Typography
- **Headings**: Manrope (700/800)
- **Body & Interface**: Inter (400/500/600)

## Radii & Elevation
- Control: 12px
- Card: 20px
- Panel: 24px
- Shadows: Card (`0 2px 4px rgb(21 38 61 / 0.02), 0 12px 28px rgb(21 38 61 / 0.05)`)
- Hover translation: up to 2px
